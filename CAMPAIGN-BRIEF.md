# Campaign brief and review

Newly prepared reusable resource, informed by the ClypChip reporting example. This is not represented as a historical planning document.

## Before launch

| Field | Complete for your campaign |
| --- | --- |
| Business problem | What customer or business need are you addressing? |
| Objective | What action should happen, for whom, and by when? |
| Audience | Location, age, relevant needs and evidence for the selection |
| Offer | Product, price, delivery conditions and available stock |
| Creative | Asset, caption, benefit, supporting proof and one call to action |
| Destination | Message inbox, product page or form; check the full journey |
| Budget | Amount, currency, dates and allocation |
| Primary result | Define likes, conversations, qualified enquiries or purchases precisely |
| Tracking | Source, attribution window, timezone and order-matching method |
| Review | Review date, decision owner and threshold for further investigation |

## Review after launch

1. Export consistent date ranges and keep original source files.
2. Choose one reporting level. Do not add campaign totals to their breakdowns.
3. Separate result types and currencies before calculating totals.
4. Calculate cost per result as matching spend divided by matching results.
5. Review audience response alongside qualified enquiries and sales quality.
6. Record changes with their dates, reasons and subsequent evidence. Do not infer causal uplift from an undocumented before-and-after comparison.
7. Match sales to orders and campaigns where possible. Record returning customers and returns separately.

Then explain why August–October sales should not be divided by August–September conversations as a verified purchase conversion rate.
